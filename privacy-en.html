Privacy Policy

Sukima Party (スキマパーティー)

そーす ("we", "us") describes in this Privacy Policy how we handle information about users of the mobile app "Sukima Party" (the "App"), in compliance with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information of Japan (APPI) and other applicable laws.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to the App and to the web pages we provide for the App.

2. Information We Collect

The App has a mode in which players pass one device around ("Single-Device Mode") and a mode in which several devices connect over the internet ("Online Mode").

In Single-Device Mode, the App sends no information off the device. Nicknames and other inputs are used only on that device.

Only when you use Online Mode, we collect the following information.

InformationDetailsHow it is collected
Device identifierAn app-scoped identifier provided by the OS (identifierForVendor on iOS, ANDROID_ID on Android). This is not an advertising identifier (IDFA / Advertising ID).Read automatically by the App and sent to our server.
Guest account dataAn account ID, an internal username and creation / update timestamps, generated automatically by our server from the device identifier.Created automatically the first time you use Online Mode.
Nickname and iconThe display name you set and the icon you choose.Entered or selected by you.
PassphraseThe word you enter to join a room.Entered by you.
In-game contentAnswers, choices, chat messages and other content sent to play the game.Entered or sent by you.
Connection dataIP address, connection time, and communication and error logs.Recorded automatically when the App connects to our server.

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, location, contacts, photos or videos, camera or microphone data, payment information, or advertising identifiers.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information only for the following purposes:

  1. To create guest accounts and identify users
  2. To connect users who entered the same passphrase in a room so they can play together online
  3. To deliver game information to other users in the same room
  4. To investigate and fix bugs and keep the server running reliably
  5. To prevent and respond to unauthorized access and abusive behavior
  6. To respond to inquiries

4. Information Shown to Other Users

Your nickname, icon and in-game content are shown on the screens of other users in the same room. Please do not enter information that can identify you, such as your real name, address or phone number, in your nickname or chat.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not provide your information to third parties without your consent, except where:

  1. Required by law
  2. Necessary to protect a person's life, body or property and it is difficult to obtain your consent
  3. Especially necessary to improve public health or promote the healthy development of children and it is difficult to obtain your consent
  4. Necessary to cooperate with a government agency or local authority performing duties prescribed by law, and obtaining your consent may impede those duties

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

6. Server Operation and Storage Location

Online Mode runs on a server that we manage, using the open-source game server software "Nakama" on infrastructure rented from a cloud server provider. The provider supplies the infrastructure under terms that do not allow it to handle the stored data, and we manage the data under our own responsibility.

Data is stored on servers located in Japan. If we start storing data outside Japan, we will update this Policy in advance to name the country and describe its personal data protection system.

7. Advertising, Analytics and Tracking

The App currently does none of the following:

  • Displaying ads or sending information to ad networks
  • Using analytics tools or third-party data collection SDKs
  • Tracking users across apps or websites owned by other companies
  • Collecting advertising identifiers (IDFA / Advertising ID)

If we introduce any of these in the future, we will update this Policy before doing so, listing the companies involved, the information sent, the purposes and how to opt out. Where consent is required by law or by app store rules, we will ask for your consent in the App first.

8. Data Retention

  • Device identifier and guest account data: Kept until you delete your account.
  • Nickname, icon, passphrase and in-game content: Used only to relay them to other users in the room and not stored on the server. They are cleared from server memory when everyone leaves the room.
  • Connection data (logs): Kept for up to 90 days from the date of recording and then deleted, unless a longer period is necessary for legal or operational reasons such as investigating abuse.

9. Deleting Your Account and Data

You can delete your guest account in the App at any time:

  1. On the title screen, tap the gear button in the top-right corner to open Settings (せってい).
  2. Tap "Delete account" (アカウントを削除), then tap "Delete" (削除する) on the confirmation screen.

Your guest account and the associated data, such as the device identifier, are deleted from the server immediately and cannot be restored. If you use Online Mode again afterwards, a new guest account will be created.

Uninstalling the App does not delete your account on the server. To delete it, follow the steps above before uninstalling, or contact us (Section 15).

Deleted data may remain in backups kept for disaster recovery for up to 30 days before being erased. We do not use data in backups for any other purpose.

10. Your Rights

Under the APPI, you may request that we notify you of the purposes of use, disclose, correct, add to or delete your data, stop using or erase it, stop providing it to third parties, or disclose records of provision to third parties. Depending on where you live, you may have similar rights under local laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA.

Please send requests to the contact in Section 15. We may ask you to verify your identity, for example by opening the App on your device. We do not charge a fee. If we cannot comply with a request under applicable law, we will tell you why.

11. Security Measures

  • We have established and published this Policy.
  • We act as the person responsible for data handling, and only we handle the data.
  • Data is stored in the cloud provider's data centers and not on our own devices unless necessary.
  • Communication between the App and the server is encrypted (TLS), and access to server administration is limited to us and protected by authentication.
  • Data is stored in Japan (Section 6).

12. Children

The App is not directed primarily at children under 13. Minors should use the App with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Parents or guardians who wish to have a child's data deleted can delete the account as described in Section 9 or contact us.

13. Users Outside Japan

If you use the App from outside Japan, your information is sent to servers in Japan and handled in accordance with Japanese law.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in law or in the App. The updated Policy takes effect when posted on this page. For changes that significantly affect your rights, we will notify you in advance in the App or on this page and obtain your consent where required by law.
